Break Check Validator

This page accepts CSV files only and extracts invalid shifts. Validation is based on the current break rules: shifts from 5 hours to under 10 hours must include one unpaid 30-minute meal break, shifts of 10 hours or more also require two paid 20-minute rest breaks, unpaid meal breaks must start after the first hour and before 5 continuous hours, and 60-minute or greater gaps are treated as separate shifts. Because this CSV only records unpaid meal breaks, required paid rest breaks are flagged as rule violations instead of being directly verified.
